How to use on the drawing
  1. Pick two points (for example pole 82W and 81E).
  2. Count blue grid squares across (ΔX) and up (ΔY). Part squares are allowed, e.g. 7.5.
  3. Tap + / − or type the two numbers.
  4. Set the real length of one square. Default 10 m matches a 10 mm grid at 1:1000.
  5. The red line is the slope distance. The answer stays pinned at the top.
